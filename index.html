Alarm and time zone guide

Times use a 24-hour clock. Flight dates and times use their airports' local time zones, including daylight saving. UTC, TZID and calendar time-zone values retain their scheduled instant. Today's date and manual alarms use the selected time-zone mode. Roster inference is a suggestion; choose your actual airport or device time zone in the Alarm tab. Check this against your actual location and official roster before enabling alarms.

Background alerts require notification permission. Check your device notification and sound settings.

Set your home base and domestic airport codes before importing a schedule. Review the departure reminder offsets for each flight.

Privacy and stored data

Only generic titles are used when enabled. Reopen the app after changing this setting to verify reservations. Existing preparation notes and PDFs are unchanged.

Privacy Policy

CrewClock stores imported schedules, alarm labels, and settings on this device. Calendar files are read to import flight events. The app does not require an account.

Use Delete All Flights and Alarms to remove schedule and alarm data. Preferences remain saved. System backups may retain app data according to your device settings. Alarm labels and routes may appear on your lock screen; control previews in system notification settings.

Paste the text copied from your monthly roster. Date headings apply to the flights below them. Departure and arrival entries on separate days are joined. Days off, reserve, layovers and training entries are excluded, as with flight calendar imports.
