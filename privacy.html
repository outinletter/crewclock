CrewClock Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-16

CrewClock processes selected rosters, schedules, preparation records, notes, alarms, document expiry dates and preferences on your device. It does not use accounts, advertising, advertising identifiers or analytics, and does not upload these records to a developer server.

Roster calendar files, PDFs and images you choose are read on your device, including on-device text recognition, and are not uploaded. iOS receives alarm titles and scheduled times so that alarms can ring. Delete all roster in Settings removes flights, their alarms and preparation lists; personal alarms, reminders, document dates and preferences remain. Device backups may retain data. Permissions can be revoked in system settings.

GitHub may process ordinary access information when hosting support and policy pages. Information you email is used to respond to inquiries. Do not send confidential rosters.
