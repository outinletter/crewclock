CrewClock Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-29

CrewClock processes selected rosters, schedules, preparation records, notes, alarms and preferences on your device. It does not use accounts, advertising, advertising identifiers or Firebase analytics, and does not upload these records to a developer server.

iOS receives alarm titles and scheduled times. Generic titles can be enabled in Settings. PDFs are shared only with destinations you select. Delete All Flights and Alarms removes schedules, preparation records and alarms; preferences remain. Device backups may retain data. Permissions can be revoked in system settings.

GitHub may process ordinary access information when hosting support and policy pages. Information you email is used to respond to inquiries. Do not send confidential rosters.
